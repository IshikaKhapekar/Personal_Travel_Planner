DAY-WISE ITINERARY

Day 1: Royal Heritage & Old City Flavors

Morning: Start early with a visit to the iconic Hawa Mahal to capture the morning light. Right after, head to Samrat Restaurant in the old city for a traditional breakfast of Pyaaz Kachori, Jalebi, and Masala Chai.
Afternoon: Explore the grand City Palace and the nearby Jantar Mantar observatory. For lunch, indulge in a traditional Rajasthani Thali at Laxmi Mishthan Bhandar (LMB) in Johari Bazar.
Evening: Stroll through the bustling lanes of Bapu Bazar for local shopping (leather goods, block prints). End your day with a visit to the beautifully illuminated Albert Hall Museum, followed by dinner at local favorite, Sethi Dhaba (famous for Beawar Paneer Tikka/local curries).
Day 2: Forts, Sunsets & Royal Views

Morning: Head out of the main city to the majestic Amber Fort. Hire a local guide to learn about its rich history. On the way back, stop for a photo opportunity at the scenic Jal Mahal (Water Palace).
Afternoon: Visit Jaigarh Fort, home to the world's largest cannon on wheels (Jaivana). Have a late lunch at a nearby dhaba or cafe overlooking the hills.
Evening: Head to Nahargarh Fort to witness a breathtaking sunset over Jaipur city. Enjoy dinner at a rooftop restaurant in the city, or try the famous Lal Maas (traditional Rajasthani mutton curry) at Handi Restaurant (veg options are also highly rated).
Day 3: Culture, Colors & Street Food Feast

Morning: Visit the vibrant Patrika Gate (Jawahar Circle), known for its exquisite colorful murals depicting Rajasthan's history. Grab breakfast at a local cafe nearby.
Afternoon: Visit the peaceful Galta Ji Temple (often called the Monkey Temple), nestled in a mountain pass. After exploring, head straight to Masala Chowk for lunch. This is an open-air food court where you can try various local street food specialties like Golgappe, Dal Baati Churma, and Kulfi in one place.
Evening: Visit the serene Birla Temple made of pure white marble. Spend your final evening doing last-minute souvenir shopping or relaxing at a heritage cafe before departure.
